# Fynder Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 20, 2026

Fynder ("Fynder", "we", "us", or "our") is a macOS PDF reader and search app developed by Gradients AI. This policy explains how Fynder handles information.

## Information Fynder accesses

Fynder accesses only the PDF files and search queries that you explicitly open or enter. Fynder processes document text and search queries locally on your Mac using on-device models.

## Information we collect

Fynder does not collect, upload, or store your PDF files, document contents, search queries, or personal information on our servers. Fynder does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics for tracking users.

Fynder does not sell, rent, or share your documents or search activity with third parties.

## Subscriptions

Fynder Pro subscriptions and free trials are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Fynder checks your subscription status on your Mac using Apple's StoreKit.

## Data stored on your Mac

Fynder may keep local app preferences and temporary processing data required to provide its features. This data stays on your Mac and can be removed by deleting the app and its associated data using macOS tools.

## Support requests

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to your request and improve support. Our website may have its own privacy practices.

## Children

Fynder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Fynder's practices change. We will post the updated policy at this URL and revise the effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through [gradientsai.com](https://gradientsai.com).
